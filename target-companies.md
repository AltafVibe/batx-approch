# Startup Target Analysis for Digital Marketing & SEO

This document contains a prioritized list of recent startups (Seed/Series A) and ranks them based on how urgently they need digital marketing and SEO services, and how easy it would be to land them as a client.

## Star Rating Guide
* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5) = **Excellent Target** (B2C or Direct Lead Gen, urgent need for SEO/Ads)
* ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5) = **Good Target** (B2B/SaaS needing organic traffic and inbound leads)
* ⭐⭐⭐ (3/5) = **Average Target** (Enterprise B2B, longer deal cycles, needs ABM and highly technical SEO)
* ⭐⭐ (2/5) = **Hard Target** (Deep tech/Manufacturing, relies more on PR and offline relationships)
